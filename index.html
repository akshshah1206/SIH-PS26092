📖 About This Platform

What is MoSJE Udyam Saarthi?

The Ministry of Social Justice & Empowerment (MoSJE) through NSFDC provides concessional financial assistance to Scheduled Caste (SC) entrepreneurs with annual family income up to ₹5 Lakhs. Funds flow via Channel Partners — State Channelizing Agencies (SCAs), Public Sector Banks (PSBs), Regional Rural Banks (RRBs), and NBFC-MFIs.

This platform eliminates guesswork by automatically matching you to the right scheme, calculating your EMI at concessional rates, and routing you to the nearest channel partner — all in one place.
